THE PROBLEM DOMAIN

A marketplace with several moving parts

The system brings the catalog, checkout, payment, and delivery process into one connected data model. A single cart can contain products from different businesses, while fulfillment is tracked separately for each business’s part of the order.

  1. 01Browse & collect

    A customer adds products to a cart, with a quantity and notes for each item.

  2. 02Place an order

    Checkout connects the cart to its dispatch zone, payment, and an optional voucher.

  3. 03Split by business

    Suborders keep each business’s status and total separate within the main order.

  4. 04Deliver & review

    Each suborder has a rider-handled delivery and can receive customer feedback.

People & permissions

Customers purchase, partners operate businesses, riders deliver, and admins oversee accounts and businesses.

Geography & fees

Zones organize businesses, riders, customer addresses, and order dispatch, with a fixed delivery fee for each zone.

FROM REQUIREMENTS TO RELATIONS

The ideas behind the model

01 / SPECIALIZATION

Shared identity, distinct roles

Every user is exactly one of Admin, Partner, Rider, or Customer. Shared account attributes stay in User, while each subtype has its own relation linked through user_id. Restaurants and pharmacies use the same approach beneath Business.

02 / MULTIVALUED ATTRIBUTES

One phone number per row

Users and businesses can have multiple phone numbers. User_Phones and Business_Phones store those values separately. The composite full name is mapped into first, middle, and last name fields.

03 / RELATIONSHIP ATTRIBUTES

The cart contains more than products

The many-to-many cart–product relationship becomes Cart_Contains_Products. Its composite key identifies an item within a cart, while quantity and customer_notes describe that particular selection.

04 / COMPOSITE IDENTITY

A suborder belongs to its order

Sub_Order uses (sub_order_number, order_id) as its primary key. Delivery and feedback carry that pair as a composite foreign key, so both refer to the correct business-specific part of an order.

Values derived from other records

The design describes the order’s status in terms of its suborder statuses. Delivery duration is defined as arrives_at − leaves_at. These are model rules recorded in Milestone 1; enforcement will belong to the implementation work.
